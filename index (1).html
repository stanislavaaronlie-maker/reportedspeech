Pengertian Aturan Tenses Keterangan Waktu Contoh Kalimat

1. Apa itu Reported Speech?

Reported Speech (atau Indirect Speech) adalah kalimat tidak langsung yang digunakan untuk menyampaikan kembali perkataan orang lain tanpa mengutip kata demi kata secara persis.

Berbeda dengan Direct Speech yang memakai tanda kutip ("..."), Reported Speech biasanya menggunakan kata penghubung seperti said that atau told me that.

2. Perubahan Tenses (Mundur Satu Langkah)

Jika kata kerja pengantarnya berbentuk lampau (seperti said), maka tenses di dalam kalimat harus berubah menjadi lebih lampau:

Direct Speech (Kalimat Langsung) Reported Speech (Tidak Langsung)
Simple Present Simple Past
Present Continuous Past Continuous
Simple Past Past Perfect
Will (Future) Would
Can Could

3. Perubahan Keterangan Waktu

  • Now (sekarang) → Then (saat itu)
  • Today (hari ini) → That day (hari itu)
  • Tomorrow (besok) → The next day (keesokan harinya)
  • Yesterday (kemarin) → The day before (sehari sebelumnya)
  • Here (di sini) → There (di sana)

4. Contoh Penggunaan

Direct: Rina said, "I study English every day."

Reported: Rina said that she studied English every day.

Direct: Budi said, "I am playing football now."

Reported: Budi said that he was playing football then.

Direct: Sarah said, "I will visit my grandma tomorrow."

Reported: Sarah said that she would visit her grandma the next day.
